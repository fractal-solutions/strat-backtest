# ICT 2022 model

Source: the supplied PDF, *Complete ICT Trading Strategy – 2022 Trading Model*
(Michael Huddleston's 2022 model). This document maps the PDF's discretionary
rules to the mechanical implementation in `src/strategies/ict/2022.js`.

## The model in the PDF

**Time (PDF's GMT table)**

| Event | EST | GMT |
|---|---|---|
| New York midnight open | 00:00 | 04:00 |
| London session open | 03:00 | 07:00 |
| New York session open | 08:00 | 12:00 |
| New York lunch | 12:00–14:00 | 16:00–18:00 |
| London session close | 12:00 | 16:00 |

**London model**

1. Establish the daily bias.
2. Mark the range high/low from the NY midnight open to the London open.
3. At the London open, wait for a **liquidity sweep** of that range. The sweep
   should raid the side *opposite* the daily bias: bullish bias raids the low,
   bearish bias raids the high.
4. After the sweep, wait for a **market-structure shift with displacement** on
   the lower timeframes (5m/3m/1m).
5. Mark the resulting **PD array**: fair-value gap, order block, inverse FVG, or
   breaker block. Use premium/discount to choose the entry zone.
6. Wait for price to retrace into the PD array and enter.
7. Stop beyond the range extreme (above the London high / below the London low),
   target the opposite side of the range. The PDF targets 1:3 or better.

**New York model**

- **If London already swept liquidity and moved away:** draw Fibonacci from the
  London low to the London high (or high to low). During the NY session, wait
  for a retracement into the **Optimal Trade Entry** (62%–79%), confirm with an
  MSS on 1m, enter in the London direction, stop beyond the London extreme,
  target the opposite side.
- **If London was range-bound:** mark the range from the NY midnight open to the
  NY open, then run the same sweep → MSS → PD-array model during the NY session.

**Liquidity the PDF cares about:** previous day high/low, previous session
high/low, previous week high/low, established 15m highs/lows, week opening gap,
day opening gap.

**Timeframes:** D1 for bias, H1 for context, M15 for liquidity/imbalance,
M5/M3/M1 for the MSS and execution.

## Mechanical mapping

| PDF rule | Code |
|---|---|
| Daily bias | Last completed daily close vs its 20-day EMA (`dailyBiasAt`) |
| Range | `range-start` to `london-open`, default 04:00–07:00 UTC |
| Liquidity sweep | Wick through the range extreme, close back inside |
| MSS | Close through the most recent swing high/low within `mss-bars` |
| Displacement | Body ≥ 55% of the bar range and range ≥ `displacement-atr` × ATR14 |
| PD array | Most recent FVG in the bias direction, else the last opposite candle |
| Premium/discount | Long entry at or below the range midpoint; short above it |
| Entry | Limit at the PD-array midpoint, `valid-bars` to fill |
| Stop | Range low − buffer (long) / range high + buffer (short) |
| Target | Opposite side of the range, subject to `min-rr` |
| NY OTE | 62%–79% retracement of the London session leg |
| NY range-bound | 04:00–12:00 range, same sweep → MSS → PD logic during NY |

`ict-2022-mtf` changes some defaults: `--london-end 12`, `--ny-end 16`,
`--bias-mode prev-day`, `--mss-bars 24`, `--swing-left 2`, `--swing-right 2`,

It adds:

## True multi-timeframe mode

`ict-2022-mtf` is the PDF's actual workflow. The HTF series supplies the daily
bias, the 04:00–07:00 range, the London sweep, and the HTF range target. The LTF
series supplies the market-structure shift, displacement, PD array, and entry.

```bash
bun run backtest --strategy ict-2022-mtf \
  --data data/m5/EURUSD.csv --tf M5 \
  --htf-data data/m15/EURUSD.csv \
  --symbol EURUSD --spread-points 10 \
  --start-date 2022-01-01 --end-date 2025-12-31
```

The engine loads the LTF as the primary `bars` (so stats, carry, and exposure
use M5 bars) and the HTF as `ctx.htfBars`. Add `--debug` to print the filter
funnel.

**Signal mode (default, PDF rules only):**

The broad sample is significantly **negative**, not positive. The best 1:3 subset

Three earlier fixes were also required: the HTF entry scan starts **after** the
HTF bar closes; the London stop is the session extreme **at entry**; and Sunday
is excluded from the daily-bias series.

| PDF rule | Implementation | Status |

| MSS with displacement on 5m/3m/1m | LTF M5, 5-bar swing, close beyond, 0.5 ATR / 40% body displacement | **Aligned in structure.** Displacement thresholds are mechanical approximations. |
| PD array: FVG, order block, inverse FVG, breaker | `findPdArray`: FVG, order block, inverse FVG, breaker; the NY range model prioritizes the breaker | **Aligned.** |

### Findings

The rule-aligned **M15-structure / M5-execution** configuration had negative gross
expectancy. Moving the range/sweep/MSS to **M5** with **H4** as a trend filter and
**D1** as the bias produced a superficially strong result — until the remaining
look-aheads were removed:

| Config | Trades | Gross R | Net R | PF | Sharpe | DSR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| M15 structure, M5 execution | 662 | −0.02 | −0.50 | 0.46 | −3.19 | 0.000 |
| M5 structure, H4 trend, D1 bias (pre-fix) | 301 | +0.71 | +0.35 | 1.74 | 0.96 | 0.000 |
| **M5 structure, H4 trend, D1 bias (fixed)** | **543** | **+0.03** | **−0.37** | **0.55** | **−2.47** | **0.000** |

**PD-array and trade-management audit (551 trades):**

| Management | Trades | Win | Mean net R | PF | Monthly Sharpe | DSR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| PDF single target | 551 | 41.4% | −0.377 | 0.534 | −0.750 | 0.000 |
| Partial 50% at 1R + breakeven | 551 | 47.0% | −0.387 | 0.433 | −1.096 | 0.000 |
| Partial + one-bar trail | 551 | 49.9% | −0.376 | 0.438 | −1.057 | 0.000 |

The breaker PD array is now included (8 additional trades). Partials raise the
win rate but cut the 1:3 tail, so PF and Sharpe get worse. The PDF's single
range target is the best of the three — and still negative.

The fixed result is significantly **negative**, not positive. The 1:3 strict
subset is breakeven (+0.015R, PF 1.02, 40 trades) with a bootstrap CI that
includes zero.

The honest conclusion: **there is no validated ICT edge in this mechanical
implementation.** The apparent edge came from look-ahead in the daily bias and
the H4 trend filter. The next work is the missing selection layer (which sweep,
MSS, PD array, and bias), the breaker/inverse-FVG branches, the previous
day/week liquidity pools, and the news filter — not more parameter tuning.

## Honest limitations

- **H1 is too coarse.** The PDF executes the MSS on 5m/3m/1m. An H1 run will
  find far fewer valid setups. Use M15 or M5 for a realistic test.
- **DST is not modelled.** The PDF's GMT table is fixed. During daylight saving,
  shift the hour flags (e.g. `--london-open 8`) or accept the mismatch.
- **Daily bias is an EMA proxy**, not a discretionary read of draw-on-liquidity.
- **PD-array selection is mechanical**: most recent FVG, else order block. A
  human would weigh premium/discount, liquidity targets, and news.
- **No news filter.** CPI/NFP can invalidate the map inside the killzone.
- **Intrabar ordering is conservative**, but bar data cannot resolve every path.
- **A first-pass strategy is not an edge.** Record every variant in
  `results/trials.json` and let the deflated Sharpe penalise the search.
